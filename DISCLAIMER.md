# Authorized use

mcp-forge builds MCP servers from observations of software and services. You are
responsible for having permission to analyze the target, and for using any
generated server within the target's terms of service and applicable law.

- Analyze apps, sites and accounts you own or are authorized to test.
- Generated servers sign in with your own credentials, supplied through
  environment variables. mcp-forge does not collect, share or store credentials.
- mcp-forge does not bypass authentication, bot protection, rate limits or other
  access controls. When it detects them, it stops rather than working around them.
- Do not publish generated servers for third-party services you do not control.
  Their private APIs and terms may forbid it.

mcp-forge relies on REA, which has its own disclaimer:
https://github.com/morluto/rea/blob/main/DISCLAIMER.md
